Foundations

Two intensive programming bootcamps — ALX and HNG — and a stack of unglamorous exercises: a Unix shell written from scratch in C, sorting algorithms, binary trees, an Airbnb backend clone built four separate times with four different stacks. This is where the fundamentals got load-bearing.

Building for people

The exercises turned into things other people would actually use: an access manager for a multi-tenant school platform, an SMS service for a small business, a mobile app that parses bank SMS to track spending automatically, another that connects students with mental health counselors.
